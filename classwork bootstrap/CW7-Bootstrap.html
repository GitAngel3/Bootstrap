Wayanad-Known for its soothing temperature all around the year, Wayanad is the ultimate destination to escape the scorching sun of North India. This place doesn’t only offer its cool temperature but along with that, it has untouched woods, misty mountains, and long spread plantations.

Munnar-Munnar, one of the most popular hill stations in Kerala lies cradled amidst the Anaimalai hills at 1600 meters. Its beautiful landscape is dotted with plantations, waterfalls, and hiking trails.

Thekkady-Enveloped with lush greenery, and diverse flora and fauna, it is a haven for nature and wildlife enthusiasts. Thekkady is home to several animals and the famous Periyar National Park.

Athirapally-The famous Athirapally waterfalls have also been compared to the great Niagara Falls. Athirapally promises to be an exhilarating experience for the nature enthusiast

Hill Places in Kerala

Munnar, Wayanad, Vagamon, Ponmudi, Idukki, Thekkady, Lakkidi, Peermedu, Malampuzha, Athirapally, Thiruvambadi, Vithura, Kalpetta and many more.

Almost heaven is how you would describe the hill stations in Kerala which is why it is popularly known as “God’s Own Country”. Kerala, located on the Southwest Malabar Coast of India is a dream getaway. It has a unique topography lying wedged between the Lakshadweep Sea and the Western Ghats of India. These immaculate hill stations rise up to elevations as high as 5000 feet and will whisk you away to a magical rendezvous with nature. The hill placs in Kerala like Munnar, Vagamon, Thekkady are blessed with a unique natural bounty of misty mountains lying in close proximity to famous rivers that are added attractions in their own right.
