FAQs

Why do electric vehicles offer an especially sporty driving sensation?
In an electric vehicle, the full range of performance and torque are available instantly. Pressing the accelerator is all that is needed for the vehicle to accelerated up to its top speed without any interruption due to gear shifts. This creates a very intense sensations and is reminiscent of motorsports. The sound composed especially for the M vehicles by Hans Zimmer supports the perceived dynamics.
